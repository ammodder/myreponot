Country/region allowances for gambling apps
Gambling apps are allowed in the countries/regions listed below with the specified limitations.
All gambling apps must also include a statement regarding responsible gambling in the description text of the app listing AND within the app that includes local contact information for third-party problem gambling support resources in addition to complying with any relevant legal and licensing requirements in relation to responsible gambling statements. Some examples of potential third-party resources are included below.
