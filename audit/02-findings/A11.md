## 1. REAL-MONEY POLICY ANALYSIS (FIRST, per TASK-006)

### 1(a) Gambling / Real-Money Games, Contests & Tournaments — exact policy text

**Source: "Real-Money Gambling, Games, and Contests", Play Console Help, https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9877032 (accessed 2026-09-20; full text: `policy-raw/text-rmg-9877032.txt`).**

The policy has exactly three doors for real money: (1) licensed Gambling Apps, (2) limited-time Google-run Pilots, (3) Gamified Loyalty Programs. Everything else is prohibited. Verbatim:

- Policy summary: *"While Google Play generally prohibits apps from facilitating real-money gambling and gaming, an exception is made for those that are licensed and approved by Google. All approved apps must be free to download, must not use In-app Billing, and must prevent access for minors and unauthorized locations."*

**Door 1 — Gambling Apps (license path).** Verbatim: *"Subject to restrictions and compliance with all Google Play policies, we allow apps that enable or facilitate online gambling in select countries, as long as the Developer completes the application process for gambling apps being distributed on Google Play, is an approved governmental operator and/or is registered as a licensed operator with the appropriate governmental gambling authority in the specified country, and provides a valid operating license in the specified country for the type of online gambling product they want to offer."*

The only allowed product types (verbatim list): *"Online Casino games / Sports Betting / Horse Racing (where regulated and licensed separately from Sports Betting) / Lotteries / Daily Fantasy Sports"*. **A skill-based tournament platform with cash prizes is not in this product list.**

**The operative prohibition (verbatim, "Other Real-Money Games, Contests, and Tournament Apps"):**

> *"For all other apps which do not meet the eligibility requirements for gambling apps noted above and are not included in the "Other Real-Money Game Pilots" noted below, we don't allow content or services that enable or facilitate users' ability to wager, stake, or participate using real money (including in-app items purchased with money) to obtain a prize of real world monetary value. This includes but is not limited to, online casinos, sports betting, lotteries, and games that accept money and offer prizes of cash or other real world value (except programs permitted under the Gamified Loyalty Programs requirements described below)."*

The policy's own **"Examples of violations"** map one-to-one onto AREENAX features (all verbatim):

1. *"Games that accept money in exchange for an opportunity to win a physical or monetary prize"* — AREENAX: paid tournament entry (`Api.kt:162-163 tournaments/{id}/join`) with prize pool split 50/30/20 (`SPEC/01-SCREENS.md:867`).
2. *"Apps that have navigational elements or features (for example, menu items, tabs, buttons, webviews, etc.) that provide a "call to action" to wager, stake, or participate in real-money games, contests or tournaments using real money, such as apps that invite users to "BET!" or "REGISTER!" or "COMPETE!" in a tournament for a chance to win a cash prize."* — AREENAX: Join/Register buttons on paid tournaments.
3. *"Apps that accept or manage wagers, in-app currencies, winnings, or deposits in order to gamble for, or obtain a physical or monetary prize."* — AREENAX: wallet deposits, withdrawals, winnings, user-to-user transfer (`Api.kt:138-145` `wallet/deposit`, `wallet/withdraw`, `wallet/transfer`).

Note: the policy's prohibition clause contains **no skill-vs-chance carve-out**. Skill games are only relevant inside Door 1 (licensed gambling products) or Door 2 (pilots). There is no general "skill games are fine" exemption in the current policy text.

**Door 2 — Pilots (verbatim):** *"We may occasionally conduct limited time pilots for certain types of real money gaming in select regions. For details, refer to this Help Center page. The Online Crane Games pilot in Japan ended on July 11, 2023. Effective July 12, 2023, Online Crane Game apps may be listed on Google Play globally subject to applicable law and certain requirements."*

→ As of 2026-09-20 the policy page names **no open pilot** for real-money skill tournaments/contests. The only named pilot (Online Crane Games, Japan) ended 2023-07-11 and its successor is crane-game-specific. **[paraphrase]** There is currently no pilot path a tournament app could apply to; the "pilot program countries" question therefore resolves to: pilots are select-regions + limited-time and none is open today.

**Even showing ads or companion features for RMG is restricted (verbatim, "Ads for Gambling or Real-Money Games…"):** an app *"must not provide gambling or real money game, lottery, or tournament support or companion functionality (for example, functionality that assists with wagering, payouts, sports score/odds/performance tracking, or management of participation funds)"*. AREENAX literally does "management of participation funds" — so even in a hypothetical stripped-down listing, the money-management feature set is named as disallowed support functionality.

### Countries where gambling apps are allowed — and Pakistan

**Source: "Country/region allowances for gambling apps", Play Console Help, https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12256011?hl=en (accessed 2026-09-20; full text: `policy-raw/text-countries-12256011.txt`).**

Verbatim opener: *"Gambling apps are allowed in the countries/regions listed below with the specified limitations. All gambling apps must also include a statement regarding responsible gambling in the description text of the app listing AND within the app…"*

The page lists **41 countries/regions** (extracted from the fetched page): Australia, Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Bulgaria, Canada, Colombia, Croatia, Czech Republic, Denmark, Ethiopia, Finland, France, Germany, Ghana, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Kenya, Latvia, Lithuania, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand, Nigeria, Norway, Panama, Portugal, Romania, Serbia, Singapore, Slovakia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Tanzania, Turkey, United Kingdom, United States.

**Pakistan is NOT on the list (verified by grep of the fetched text: 0 hits for "Pakistan"). India is also absent.** So even the licensed-gambling path (Door 1) is unavailable for Pakistan distribution, regardless of product type. Note this list governs the *licensed gambling* door only; it does not open a door for unlicensed real-money contests anywhere.

### 1(b) Payments policy — manual bank transfers vs Play billing; where the line is

**Source: "Understanding Google Play's Payments policy", Play Console Help, https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10281818?hl=en (accessed 2026-09-20; full text: `policy-raw/text-payments-10281818.txt`).**

- *"Google Play's billing system is required for developers offering in-app purchases of digital goods and services distributed on Google Play."*
- In scope of Play billing (verbatim): *"Digital items (such as virtual currencies, extra lives, additional playtime, add-on items, characters, or avatars) / Subscription services… / App functionality or content… / Cloud software and services…"*
- Out of scope of Play billing (verbatim): *"Purchases or rentals of physical goods (such as groceries, clothing, home appliances, or electronics) / Purchases of physical services (such as transportation services, airfare, gym memberships, or food delivery) / Payment of a credit card or utility bill"*
- Explicit gambling carve-out (verbatim): *"Google Play's billing system must not be used for peer-to-peer payments, content that facilitates online gambling, or any product category deemed unacceptable under Google's Payments Center Content Policies."*

**The line for AREENAX:** AREENAX's manual receipt-upload deposits (bank/JazzCash/EasyPaisa) never go through any in-app purchase mechanism at all — the user pays an external real-world counterparty and uploads proof. That is a real-world money movement (closest to "physical services"/P2P), not an in-app purchase of a digital good, so the Payments policy per se does not force it into Play billing. However, this does not help on Play, because the **RMG policy independently prohibits the entire money→prize loop** (1(a) above) and separately states approved gambling apps *"must NOT … use Google Play In-app Billing"*. Also relevant (verbatim): *"Can I distribute my app on other Android app stores or my website? — Yes, you can distribute your app however you like. As an open ecosystem… Android gives developers the freedom and flexibility to distribute their apps on other Android app stores, directly from a website, or through device preloads, all without using Google Play's billing system."*

Where the physical/real-world line sits, verbatim FAQ answers: *"Should apps that provide goods or services like insurance, stock trades, investment consulting, or tax preparation and filing assistance use Google Play's billing system? — No."* and *"As long as Google Play's billing system isn't available in a particular country, the Payments policy's requirement to use Google Play's billing system does not apply in that country."* **[paraphrase]** In short: real-world services and regulated financial transactions sit outside Play billing; in-app purchases of digital goods/features sit inside it; consumption-only apps are allowed ("Google Play allows any app to be consumption-only, even if it is part of a paid service").

### 1(c) What this means for AREENAX — VERDICT

**Verdict: AREENAX as-is (deposits → paid entry → cash prizes 50/30/20 → withdrawals/transfer) cannot be listed on Google Play — in any country, including Pakistan.** The deciding text, verbatim (source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9877032, accessed 2026-09-20):

> *"…we don't allow content or services that enable or facilitate users' ability to wager, stake, or participate using real money (including in-app items purchased with money) to obtain a prize of real world monetary value."*

combined with the named violation example *"Games that accept money in exchange for an opportunity to win a physical or monetary prize"* and *"Apps that accept or manage wagers, in-app currencies, winnings, or deposits in order to gamble for, or obtain a physical or monetary prize."* The app is not a licensed gambling operator, the product type is absent from the allowed gambling-product list, Pakistan is not in the country allowance list (12256011), and no pilot for real-money skill tournaments is open (9877032).

**This is a hard BLOCKER for the "Play readiness" goal — and explicitly NOT a blocker for the current distribution model.** AREENAX is distributed today as an APK from its own website, where Google Play policies (including RMG and Payments) do not apply; Google's own payments FAQ affirms website distribution: *"Yes, you can distribute your app however you like."* (10281818, accessed 2026-09-20). Caveat for the owner: Google is rolling out **Android developer verification for ALL Android apps, including those distributed outside Play** — per the official blog *"Android developer verification: Rolling out to all developers"* (android-developers.googleblog.com, Mar 30, 2026) and the developer.android.com summary snippet *"Google Play automatically registers 99% of apps. Use Play Console to manually register remaining apps or those distributed outside Google Play."* (both in `policy-raw/s7-dev-verification.json` + prepared `e95014e0.json`, snippets only — see NOT VERIFIED). Identity verification will thus reach web-APK distribution too; legal status of paid-entry skill tournaments in Pakistan is a separate, non-Play question for local counsel (NOT VERIFIED here).

Play listing with real money REMOVED** | RMG prohibition lifts only if no real-money stake→prize loop exists. | Large product surgery: remove deposits/withdrawals/transfer/entry fees/cash prizes (≈ wallet + entry-fee + prize UI flows); keep tournaments as free, no-cash-reward play **[paraphrase]**.
